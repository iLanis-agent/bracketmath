Bracket Math

Eleven teams signed up. Nobody can say how many games that is.

Boundary: arithmetic only Bracket Math counts byes, rounds and games from the numbers you type. It does not seed teams, pick a format, or say which format is better.

The bracket truth

11 teams: bracket of 16, 5 byes, 3 first-round games, 4 rounds, 10 games in all. Single elimination always plays one game fewer than the teams.

The league truth

6 teams, played once: 15 games over 5 rounds, 3 games per round, every team plays 5. Odd team counts add a sit-out round.

The clock truth

You enter courts and game length. 10 games on 2 courts at 45 min each: 5 time slots, 3 h 45 min of play.
